IronFit is an informal, volunteer-run community workout group among friends — not a registered business and not a professional fitness service. The app is built and run by volunteers. Contact: ironfituae.app@gmail.com

By ticking the box during onboarding you agree to these Terms, the Privacy Policy and the Waiver. You can read all three any time under Profile → Legal.

1. Who can use it. IronFit is private and invitation-based. You need to train with the community and have your registration approved by a coach or administrator — creating an account doesn't grant access on its own. You must be 18 or older to hold an account. Where the athlete is under 18, a parent or guardian holds and uses the account on their behalf: the parent accepts these Terms, the Privacy Policy and the Waiver, and the athlete's own details — including date of birth, which sets the youth training rate — are recorded on it.

2. Your account. Keep your login details to yourself; you're responsible for what happens under your account. Tell us if you think someone else has access.

3. What the app is. A tool for recording training, nutrition and progress, and for coaches to program for the group. It isn't medical advice. Calorie and macro targets are estimates from the details you enter, and individual needs vary widely. Nutrition data comes from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts — Open Food Facts is maintained by the public and can be wrong, including on allergens, so check the physical label if it matters. Talk to a doctor before starting or significantly changing how you train or eat.

4. Health. The app doesn't collect or store health conditions, injuries or medical history — deliberately. If something affects your training, tell a coach directly, before you train and whenever it changes. That's part of the Waiver. The one exception is your emergency contact, which we do store.

5. Training is at your own risk. Covered by the Waiver you accept at onboarding. You decide what's right for you on any given day and can scale or skip anything. Stop and get help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, severe breathlessness, or notice dark urine after training.

6. What other members see. Your name, photo, bio, join date, personal records, your scores on the group's workouts, and whether you've marked a workout as done — there's no setting to hide these. The date you say you did a workout is shown only to you and the coaches. Workouts you write for yourself stay private unless you share them, and announcing a personal record in the feed is your choice and off by default. Your nutrition, weigh-ins, measurements and emergency contact are never shown to other members. Coaches and administrators see everything you record.
