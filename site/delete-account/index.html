Delete your IronFit account

You can delete your account and its data yourself, from inside the app:

  1. Open IronFit and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll to Danger zone at the bottom.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

What is deleted

Your profile, training log, personal records, food diary, weight history, bookings, guest records and your sign-in identity. This happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What is kept

Payment records are kept for the gym's bookkeeping, with your name replaced by a placeholder. Signed waivers are kept for the period the law requires, as described in the Privacy Policy.

If you no longer have the app

Email ironfituae.app@gmail.com from the Google account you registered with, with the subject "Delete my IronFit account". We will confirm within 7 days.
